## 6. 0.1 by hand

- FP32: `0 01111011 10011001100110011001101` → 0.100000001490
- BF16: `0 01111011 1001101` → 0.100097656250
- FP8 E4M3: `0 0011 101` → 0.101562500000

For this experiment I would train in BF16, retaining FP32 accumulation/master state where needed. BF16 has FP32-like exponent range and therefore preserves small gradients better than FP16-like formats. Raw FP8 E4M3 is too coarse for an unscaled end-to-end cast; FP8 training needs scale management or a hybrid recipe.
